# The Wind Waker — Opening 30 Minutes Canon Reference

A scene-by-scene canonical breakdown of the first ~30 minutes of *The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker*, focused on the events covered by **Book 1 (Outset Island birthday & Aryll's kidnapping)** and **Book 2 (the first attempt on the Forsaken Fortress)**.

This file complements `wind_waker_canon.md` (which covers the overall plot/characters) — the goal here is *visual and sequential precision* for picture-book prompts.

---

## BOOK 1 — Link's Birthday on Outset Island

### Canonical Scene Order

1. Link is asleep on top of **Aryll's Lookout** (the wooden watchtower on the eastern beach of Outset Island). He did NOT sleep in his bedroom — he climbed the lookout the night before and dozed off.
2. **Aryll climbs the ladder up the lookout** and wakes him by calling/yelling at him. She reminds him it's his birthday and Grandma is waiting at home.
3. Link climbs/jumps down from the lookout and walks back to **Grandma's House** (a stilted, thatched-roof wooden cottage on the western side of the village).
4. Inside, on the upper floor, **Grandma gives Link the Hero's Clothes** — the green tunic and pointed cap. She explains it is the Outset custom for boys to wear green when they come of age, in honor of the legendary Hero of Time. Link changes outfits.
5. Link returns to Aryll's Lookout. Aryll, waiting at the top, **gives him her Telescope** as a birthday loan ("just for today!"). It is her most prized possession.
6. **Telescope sequence:** Aryll points out the Rito mailman **Quill** standing by the postbox (down on the beach). Link aims the telescope and zooms in on Quill. Quill suddenly looks up — Link follows his gaze.
7. **The giant bird (Helmaroc King) appears** in the sky, carrying a young blonde girl (Tetra) in its talons. A pirate ship below fires cannonballs at it. One cannonball strikes the bird in the face; it drops Tetra, who plummets into the **Forest of Fairies** at the top of the western cliff.
8. The pirate captain **Gonzo** (a huge bald pirate with a single-tooth grin) bursts into the village from the woods, frantic, calling Tetra's name. Link is recruited to help find her.
9. **Sword Training:** Link goes to **Orca's house** (eastern side of the village, a single small cottage Orca shares with his brother Sturgeon). Orca lives on the **first floor**; Sturgeon on the second. Orca trains Link in five sword techniques, then gives him the **Hero's Sword**. (This is the ONLY way to get the sword — it is from Orca, NOT Sturgeon, NOT Grandma.)
10. Link climbs the cliff path on the eastern side of the village, crosses a **wooden suspension bridge** spanning a chasm, and enters the **Forest of Fairies** on the western cliff.
11. In the forest, Link fights several **green Bokoblins** and finds **Tetra hanging unconscious from a tree branch**. The branch snaps; she falls and wakes up unharmed. Gonzo arrives, relieved.
12. Tetra brushes herself off and runs off ahead. Link, Tetra, and Gonzo head back across the suspension bridge toward the village.
13. **The kidnapping** happens here, on or just past the bridge: Aryll is on the village side of the bridge waving to Link. The Helmaroc King swoops down from the sky, **grabs Aryll in his talons** (mistaking her for Tetra — both have similar pointy ears and blonde hair), and flies off toward the horizon. Link runs after them and almost falls off the cliff; **Tetra catches his arm just in time**.
14. Link begs Tetra to take him to wherever the bird went. Tetra refuses at first. **Quill the Rito** arrives and convinces her. Tetra says: bring me a shield first.
15. Link returns to Grandma's House and takes the **Hero's Shield** down from where it hangs on the upper-floor wall (a family heirloom). Grandma, weeping silently, gives it to him with her blessing.
16. Link walks down to the village dock; **a farewell scene** plays — Grandma watching from the patio, the villagers gathered. Link boards Tetra's pirate ship.

### Key Visual Locations (for prompts)

- **Aryll's Lookout** — A tall, narrow wooden tower (essentially a stationary crow's nest on stilts) on the eastern shore beach. A vertical wooden ladder runs up one side. Top platform has a low railing. Seagulls circle around it. Grass and sand around the base, ocean directly behind.
- **Grandma's House** — Two-floor wooden cottage on stilts, painted timber posts, thatched roof with decorative gable carvings. Stands on the western hillside above the village beach. Has a back patio overlooking the Great Sea. Interior: warm wood, a hearth/cooking pot, a ladder to the upper floor, the Hero's Shield hanging decoratively on the upstairs wall.
- **Orca and Sturgeon's House** — A modest single cottage on the eastern side of the village. Orca's room (first floor) has wooden floor planks and weapon racks; he is an old swordmaster with a long white beard, blue/green robe, weather-worn face.
- **The Suspension Bridge** — A long, narrow wooden plank-and-rope bridge spanning a deep chasm between the eastern village cliff and the western Forest of Fairies cliff. Frayed ropes, planks of varying lengths. Drops to surf-pounded rocks below.
- **The Forest of Fairies** — A small, enclosed grove on the cliff's western plateau. Tall trees with large twisted branches, dappled sunlight, soft mossy ground, scattered flowers. Tetra falls into one specific tree at the center.
- **The Village** — A small cluster of stilted wooden houses on a curving sandy beach, palm-like trees, a wooden dock extending into a calm cove. Outset Island itself is shaped like two grass-topped hills connected by the village beach.

### Characters in Book 1 — Visual Notes

- **Link (pre-tunic, pages 1–4ish)**: blue shirt with red lobster motif, orange shorts/pants, no cap. Barefoot or simple sandals.
- **Link (post-tunic, pages 5+)**: classic green tunic, long pointed green cap, white undershirt with long sleeves, white tights, brown belt, brown boots. NO sword/shield until after Orca / after Grandma's gift.
- **Aryll**: light blue tropical sundress with red-pink hibiscus flower at the collar/strap, bare feet or simple sandals. Long blonde hair tied back, big eyes. Telescope is brass/wood, about as long as her forearm.
- **Grandma (Avia)**: small elderly woman, hunched, gray hair in a bun, wearing a long muted-rose/pink dress with an apron. Kind, sad eyes.
- **Tetra (post-rescue)**: small but tough-looking blonde girl, hair in a high bun with strands falling over her face, blue bandana around her neck/forehead, white sleeveless shirt, blue shorts, brown belt, no shoes (or simple). Knife at her hip. Hands on hips, smirking.
- **Quill**: a Rito (humanoid bird-person), tall and slender, indigo/dark blue plumage, long white pointed beak, postman uniform with a satchel. Wings folded.
- **Gonzo**: enormous pirate, completely bald, single tooth, pink/red bandana, bare-chested with suspenders, baggy pants. Looks scary but is a softie.
- **Orca**: tall, lean, very old swordmaster. Long flowing white hair and beard, deep-set eyes, blue or teal robes, holds a long spear/naginata when training. Stoic.
- **Helmaroc King**: gigantic dark bird with a glossy black-and-white mask of riveted iron plate covering his entire face (only beak protruding), yellow glowing eye-slits in the mask, large dark feathered body (deep purple-black plumage), enormous brown wings, sharp talons. He is BIRD-shaped, not dragon — think a colossal vulture/condor with a steel knight's helm strapped to his face.

## BOOK 2 — The First Forsaken Fortress (Failed Rescue)

### Canonical Scene Order

1. Link sails away from Outset on **Tetra's Pirate Ship** (large wooden caravel-style vessel with a tall mast, big main sail, skull-and-crossbones flag, gold dragon figurehead). Aryll is being held at the **Forsaken Fortress** in the far northwestern corner of the Great Sea.
2. **Below decks**, Link meets **Niko**, a goofy thin pirate, who makes Link complete a swinging-rope obstacle course (over a pit) as initiation. Link receives the **Spoils Bag**.
3. **Approach to the fortress (night):** The pirate ship anchors at a distance. The Forsaken Fortress dominates the horizon — a massive, dark, ramshackle stone citadel rising directly out of the sea on a small rocky island. It is roughly **pentagonal in layout**, with five tall stone towers ringing a central courtyard. Each tower has a **giant searchlight** mounted on top. A central tall keep with broken/jagged walls. Cannons jut from the parapets. The whole structure is **dark gray/black stone**, asymmetrical, with crooked square windows and cracked plank doors. Torches around the base flicker orange.
   - **NOTE:** The fortress is NOT explicitly skull-shaped (that's a misconception). It's a brutalist, blocky, pentagonal stone castle — its menace comes from scale, darkness, and the searchlights, not from skull motifs. (Skull motifs appear inside as decorative torches, not on the exterior.)
   - The **searchlights** are **bright white/pale yellow circles of light** sweeping across the courtyard floor and the dark sea. They look like spotlight beams from old prison-yard search towers. Each is operated by a Bokoblin standing on a small platform on top of a tower.
4. **The pirates explain their plan:** they cannot sail close enough — the searchlights and cannons would sink them. So they will **launch Link in a barrel from a catapult** mounted on the ship, aiming through a high window of the fortress.
5. **The catapult mechanism:** the ship has a **raised wooden platform/superstructure at the front** (bow) with a torsion-spring catapult bucket. Link climbs into a wooden barrel, the pirates seal it, load it into the catapult arm, the arm winds back, and **fires Link straight at the fortress wall**. Tetra is the one aiming.
6. **Link does NOT keep his sword for the launch.** Either it is taken by the pirates or it falls out of his belt during the rough flight. Either way, due to **Tetra's poor aim**, the barrel slams against the fortress's outer wall and **shatters**; Link falls down the wall into the seawater inside the fortress's flooded base. **His sword separates from him during the impact** and lands on an upper floor of the fortress.
7. Link swims/climbs out of the water onto a stone landing inside the fortress. **He has no sword.** Tetra (via the **Pirate's Charm** — a small stone she slipped into his pocket — appears as a hologram and apologizes for the rough landing.
8. **Stealth section:** Link sneaks through the fortress courtyard hiding under a barrel (a literal large wooden barrel he wears like a costume, with eyeholes), avoiding the searchlight beams. Searchlights catch a moving target → Bokoblins spot him → Moblins (large brown pig-boar warriors with spears) come and throw him in a cell.
9. (Optional escape and sword retrieval — for picture-book purposes the failure can be straight-line.)
10. **Helmaroc King capture cutscene:** As Link climbs the fortress's central tower to the top, he steps onto the upper deck of a **broken, half-buried wooden ship grounded on the roof** (yes — there's a wrecked sailing ship sitting on top of the fortress as a structural feature). The Helmaroc King descends from the sky, snatches Link in his talons/beak, and carries him up to the very top of the central keep.
11. There, Link sees **a hooded man in dark robes** sitting silently — the first appearance of **Ganondorf**, though Link doesn't know who he is. Ganondorf gestures dismissively. The Helmaroc King throws Link far out to sea.
12. Link plummets into the dark ocean. The screen fades.
13. **Link wakes up in a small red boat at sunrise.** A voice is calling his name. The boat introduces itself as **the King of Red Lions**: a small wooden sailing dinghy carved to resemble a red dragon, with a snarling dragon-head prow, golden trim, no sail yet. This is Link's **first meeting** with the King of Red Lions — it occurs OUT AT SEA, AFTER the failed rescue, NOT on Outset Island.
14. The King of Red Lions tells Link about Ganon, the world, and his quest. He brings Link to **Windfall Island** (a bustling town built on a roughly circular island — colorful buildings, windmill, crowded marketplace, lighthouse — completely unlike Outset's rural quietude).
15. On Windfall, Link must **buy the Sail** for 80 Rupees from the merchant **Zunari** (an Inuit-coded trader in a thick blue parka, square-faced, kind). Once purchased, Link installs the white triangular sail on the King of Red Lions' mast. This is when the dinghy becomes a sailable boat and Link's voyage truly begins.

### Key Visual Locations (for prompts)

- **Tetra's Pirate Ship (deck)** — Large wooden caravel, weathered planks, central mast with black sail and skull-and-crossbones, **gold-painted dragon figurehead** at the bow, **catapult bucket on raised foredeck platform** at the front, lanterns hanging on lines, cannons along the rails. Crew of rough-looking pirates.
- **Forsaken Fortress (exterior)** — Massive dark gray-black stone fortress on a tiny rock island in stormy seas. Five irregular stone towers around a central keep, tops crenellated, each tower topped with a wide circular searchlight rig (looks like a giant industrial spotlight on a swivel). Crooked square windows. Heavy plank doors. Cannons jutting from walls. Torches flicker. Sky is dark; clouds; moonlight; the sea below is choppy and inky teal-black.
- **Forsaken Fortress searchlight beam** — A wide cone of pale white-yellow light projecting from the top of each tower, sweeping in slow arcs across the courtyard floor or the sea. The light circle is sharp-edged where it hits stone.
- **Forsaken Fortress courtyard (interior)** — Open stone courtyard with raised walkways around the perimeter, partially flooded with seawater at the base, scattered wooden barrels and crates, oil lanterns, brown Moblins patrolling with spears, Bokoblins on the searchlight platforms above.
- **Top of Forsaken Fortress** — A grounded, wrecked wooden sailing ship lies broken on the roof of the central keep — broken masts, splintered hull, crow's nest tipped on its side. Stone pinnacles around it. The hooded silhouette of Ganondorf sits on a stone seat.
- **King of Red Lions** — A small (one- to two-person) wooden sailing dinghy, hull painted red with gold trim. The bow is carved into the head of a snarling red dragon: open jaws, golden teeth, sharp horns swept back, big yellow eyes. A single short mast in the middle for the sail (initially missing). Wooden bench seat behind the dragon-head.
- **Windfall Island** — A medium-sized circular island packed with colorful stucco-and-wood buildings, narrow cobbled streets, a stone windmill, a lighthouse, a town square with a tree, a busy harbor with multiple docks. Far brighter and busier than Outset.

### Characters New in Book 2 — Visual Notes

- **Niko**: thin, gangly pirate, tall blue triangular hat, blue shirt, big nose, goofy expression.
- **Bokoblin (Forsaken Fortress)**: small gray-skinned goblin, ~Link's height, hunched, wearing a loincloth, holds a stick or small sword. Pointed ears, beady black eyes, wide jagged mouth. (Different from green forest Bokoblins — these are gray/desaturated.)
- **Moblin (Forsaken Fortress)**: huge brown-furred pig-boar warrior, tall and broad, snout with tusks, wearing leather armor and a metal helmet, carries a long spear and a lantern. Patrols slowly. Throws Link in jail if it sees him.
- **King of Red Lions (the boat)** — see above; speaks in a calm, dignified deep voice. The dragon head doesn't visibly move when speaking; the boat just talks while the head looks fierce and still.
- **Ganondorf (first glimpse)**: a tall hooded figure in a long dark robe, face entirely shadowed under the cowl, sitting motionless on the fortress roof. Only the silhouette and a glint of yellow/orange eyes visible. He doesn't speak in this scene — only gestures.
- **Zunari (Windfall merchant)**: short, stout, wrapped in a thick blue parka with white fur trim, square wooden expression, kindly. Stands next to a stall on the Windfall plaza.

## CRITICAL CORRECTIONS / COMMON MISCONCEPTIONS

These are points where fan summaries (and even some wikis) get sloppy — pin these down for accuracy:

1. **Link wakes up on Aryll's Lookout, NOT in his bedroom.** He slept on top of the watchtower the night before. Aryll wakes him *there*.
2. **Aryll gives him the telescope at the lookout AFTER Link returns from getting the green tunic from Grandma.** Not before. Sequence: lookout (wake) → home (tunic) → lookout (telescope) → bird sighting.
3. **The sword is from Orca, not Sturgeon, not Grandma.** Sturgeon lives upstairs in the same house but only gives information; Grandma gives the *shield*.
4. **Aryll is kidnapped at/near the suspension bridge** between the village and the Forest of Fairies, NOT at the lookout tower and NOT at the beach. She's on the village side waving as Link returns from the forest.
5. **The Helmaroc King mistakes Aryll for Tetra** because of her pointed ears and blonde hair — this is canonically why she's taken.
6. **The catapult is on the FRONT (bow) of Tetra's pirate ship**, on a raised platform.
7. **Link loses his sword during the barrel-launch impact**, not before; the pirates do not disarm him. Tetra's poor aiming causes the crash that knocks the sword loose.
8. **Link does NOT meet the King of Red Lions on Outset Island.** First meeting is OUT AT SEA after being thrown from the Forsaken Fortress, at sunrise.
9. **The sail is bought on Windfall Island** from Zunari, AFTER the King of Red Lions brings Link there. Not on Outset.
10. **The Forsaken Fortress is pentagonal, dark stone, with five searchlight towers — NOT skull-shaped.** Skulls appear only inside as decorative torch motifs. The exterior menace is brutalist scale + searchlights + cannons + ramshackle construction.
11. **Searchlight beams are pale white/yellow** (industrial spotlight color), not red, green, or any colored beam.
12. **There is a wrecked wooden ship grounded on top of the central keep** of the Forsaken Fortress. The Helmaroc King carries Link up there. Ganondorf is sitting beside this wreckage in his first appearance.
