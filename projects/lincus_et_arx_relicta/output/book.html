Link and the Forsaken Fortress

Nox erat. Nāvis per mare obscūrum nāvigāvit.

It was night. The ship sailed on the dark sea.

1

Lincus arcem obscūram spectāvit. Soror eius intus erat.

Link looked at the dark fortress. His sister was inside.

2

Tetra ad eum vēnit. "Audī! Cōnsilium habēmus."

Tetra came to him. "Listen. We have a plan."

3

"Ascende in dolium! Tē super mūrum iaciēmus!"

"Climb in the barrel. We will throw you over the wall!"

4

Lincus ascendit. Pīrātae tormentum ēmīsērunt.

Link climbed in. The pirates fired the cannon.

5

Dolium per āera volāvit. Lincus sē arctē tenuit!

The barrel flew through the air. Link clung tight!

6

Dolium in arce cecidit. Lincus cautē prōspexit.

The barrel landed inside the fortress. Link peeked out.

7

Lūx rubra per terram cucurrit. Lincus sē cēlāvit!

A red light moved across the floor. Link hid!

8

Lincus praeter Moblīnum dormientem rēpsit.

Link crept past a sleeping Moblin.

9

Per scālās ascendit. Sūrsum et sūrsum.

Up the stairs he climbed. Up and up.

10

Vōcēs audīvit. Vōcēs flentēs.

He heard voices. Crying voices.

11

Arullam post cancellōs vīdit. "Lince!"

He saw Aryll behind the bars. "Link!"

12

Lincus cancellōs prehendit. Tōtīs vīribus trāxit.

Link grabbed the bars. He pulled with all his strength.

13

Subitō — umbra magna cecidit!

Suddenly — a great shadow fell on him!

14

Avis Magna Lincum unguibus rapuit!

The Great Bird grabbed Link in its talons!

15

Avis sūrsum volāvit. Lincum trāns mare portāvit.

The bird flew up. It carried Link far over the sea.

16

Tum avis eum dēmīsit. Lincus in mare cecidit.

Then the bird threw him down. Link fell into the sea.

17

Lentē in aquam frīgidam dēscendit.

Down he sank in the cold dark water.

18

In lītore expergēfactus est. Aurōra rosea erat.

He woke up on a quiet beach. The dawn was pink.

19

Parva nāvis locūta est! "Rēx Rubrōrum Leōnum sum."

The little boat spoke! "I am the King of Red Lions."

20

"Soror mea in arce est! Redīre dēbeō!"

"My sister is in the fortress! I must go back!"

21

Rēx eum spectāvit. "Nōndum."

The King looked at him. "Not yet."

22

Lincus sēdit. Caput dēmīsit. Vīctus erat.

Link sat down. He hung his head. He had failed.

23

Nāvis lentē ēnāvigāvit. "Nōndum. Sed iterum temptābimus."

The boat sailed slowly out to sea. "Not yet. But we will try again."

24
