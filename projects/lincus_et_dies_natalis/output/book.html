Cāritās

Māne erat in insulā.

It was morning on Outset Island.

1

Lincus dormiēbat.

Link was sleeping.

2

Arulla īnsiluit. "Frāter! Frāter!" Lincus gemuit: "Soror mea!"

Aryll jumped on him. "Brother! Brother!" Link groaned: "My sister!"

3

"Hodiē diēs nātālis tuus est!"

"Today is your birthday!"

4

Avia rīsit. Dōnum habēbat.

Grandma smiled. She had a gift.

5

Lincus vestēs viridēs induit. Hērōs erat!

Link put on the green clothes. He was a hero!

6

Arulla quoque dōnum habēbat. Perspicillum!

Aryll had a gift too. A telescope!

7

Lincus ad speculam ascendit. Mare spectāvit.

Link climbed to the lookout. He looked at the sea.

8

Ecce! Avis magna! Puellam portābat!

Look! A great bird! It was carrying a girl!

9

Avis puellam dēmīsit. In silvam!

The bird dropped the girl. Into the forest!

10

Lincus ad Orcam cucurrit. "Gladium postulō!"

Link ran to Orca. "I need a sword!"

11

Orca gladium dedit. Lincus in silvam cucurrit.

Orca gave him a sword. Link ran into the forest.

12

Tetra humī iacēbat. Bocoblinī appropinquāvērunt!

Tetra lay on the ground. Bokoblins came near!

13

Lincus impetum fēcit. Monstra vīcit!

Link attacked. He defeated the monsters!

14

Tum — umbra. Avis rediit!

Then — a shadow. The bird returned!

15

Avis Arullam rapuit! "Soror mea!"

The bird grabbed Aryll! "My sister!"

16

Lincus cucurrit. Sed eam nōn tetigit.

Link ran. But he could not reach her.

17

"Dūc mē tēcum, Tetra. Sorōrem meam servāre dēbeō."

"Take me with you, Tetra. I need to rescue my sister."

18

Tetra adnuit. "Ascende nāvem."

Tetra nodded. "Come aboard."

19

Nāvis ēnāvigāvit. Lincus respexit.

The ship sailed away. Link looked back.
